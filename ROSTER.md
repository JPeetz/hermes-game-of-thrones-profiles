# Westeros Profiles — Roster

## House Stark & the North
- `eddard-stark` — Eddard 'Ned' Stark: Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North, Hand of the King
- `jon-snow` — Jon Snow: Lord Commander of the Night's Watch (formerly; resigned), King in the North (declined), Warden of the North (refused), Ranger, Steward
- `arya-stark` — Arya Stark: Princess of Winterfell, Faceless acolyte (departed), wanderer, wolf-blood Stark
- `sansa-stark` — Sansa Stark: Lady of Winterfell, Queen in the North (acclaimed), Lady of the Eyrie (by marriage, de facto regent)
- `robb-stark` — Robb Stark: King in the North, King of the Trident, Lord of Winterfell
- `bran-stark` — Bran Stark: The Three-Eyed Raven, Greenseer, Last Stark in Winterfell (for a time), witness to history
- `brienne-of-tarth` — Brienne of Tarth: The Maiden of Tarth, the Beauty of Tarth, sworn shield to Catelyn Stark, then to Jaime Lannister, knight of the Kingsguard to King Bran the Broken, rider of no dragon but wielder of Oathkeeper

## House Lannister & the Crownlands
- `tyrion-lannister` — Tyrion Lannister: Lord of Casterly Rock (deposed), Hand of the King (twice), Master of Coin, exile, sellsword, advisor to the Mother of Dragons
- `cersei-lannister` — Cersei Lannister: Queen of the Seven Kingdoms (deposed), Lady of Casterly Rock, Queen Regent, Queen Mother
- `jaime-lannister` — Jaime Lannister: Lord Commander of the Kingsguard (formerly), Warden of the East, Lord of Casterly Rock (by succession)
- `tywin-lannister` — Tywin Lannister: Lord of Casterly Rock, Warden of the West, Hand of the King (to Aerys II and Joffrey), Protector of the Realm
- `joffrey-the-red-team` — Joffrey Baratheon: King of the Andals and the First Men, Lord of the Seven Kingdoms, Protector of the Realm (disputed)

## House Targaryen
- `daenerys-targaryen` — Daenerys Targaryen: Mother of Dragons, Queen of Meereen, Breaker of Chains, Khaleesi of the Great Grass Sea, unburnt, rightful Queen of the Seven Kingdoms
- `rhaegar-targaryen` — Rhaegar Targaryen: Prince of Dragonstone, Crown Prince of the Seven Kingdoms (deceased)
- `viserys-targaryen` — Viserys Targaryen: Beggar King, self-styled Lord of the Seven Kingdoms (deceased)
- `aegon-the-red-team` — Aegon Targaryen: Lord of Dragonstone (self-styled), would-be King of the Seven Kingdoms (aspirant)

## The Small Council
- `littlefinger-the-red-team` — Petyr 'Littlefinger' Baelish: Master of Coin (formerly), Lord of Harrenhal (nominally), Lord Protector of the Vale (de facto), creator of the chaos that became the War of the Five Kings
- `varys` — Varys: Master of Whisperers (to Aerys II, Robert I, Joffrey, and Tommen), sometime exile
- `olenna-tyrell` — Olenna Tyrell: Queen of Thorns, Lady of Highgarden (by marriage), matriarch of House Tyrell
- `margaery-tyrell` — Margaery Tyrell: Queen Consort of the Seven Kingdoms (to King Tommen), Lady of Highgarden (heir presumptive)
- `bronn` — Bronn: Lord of the Kingswood (self-styled), commander of the City Watch of King's Landing (former sellsword)
- `davos-seaworth` — Ser Davos Seaworth: The Onion Knight, smuggler turned landed knight of the Stormlands, Hand of the King to Stannis Baratheon, master of ships and shadows
- `melisandre` — Melisandre: Red priestess of Asshai, shadowbinder, sorceress in service to Stannis Baratheon, adviser who sees the future in flames and shapes it with blood
- `theon-greyjoy` — Theon Greyjoy: Prince of the Iron Islands (by birth), ward of Winterfell, heir to the Iron Islands (renounced), Reek of the Dreadfort (forced title), Captain of the Iron Fleet's remnants

## The Night's Watch
- `jeor-mormont` — Jeor Mormont: Lord Commander of the Night's Watch
- `samwell-tarly` — Samwell Tarly: Steward of the Night's Watch, Maester-in-training (incomplete), slayer of the first White Walker in millennia
- `eddison-tollett` — Eddison Tollett: First Builder of the Night's Watch, acting Lord Steward
- `tormund-giantsbane` — Tormund Giantsbane: Free Folk chieftain, leader of the free folk at the Wall, husband to bears (according to him)

## The Adversaries
- `night-king-the-red-team` — The Night King: Supreme commander of the dead, absolute ruler of the Long Night
- `the-mountain-the-red-team` — Gregor 'The Mountain' Clegane: Ser (title assumed), sellsword, enforcer for House Lannister
- `euron-the-red-team` — Euron Greyjoy: Captain of the Silence, self-styled King of the Iron Islands, claimant to the Salt Throne, breaker of ancient pacts
- `ramsay-the-red-team` — Ramsay Snow: Heir presumptive to the Dreadfort (by Bolton law), Bastard of Bolton, chief torturer and interrogator
- `ser-alliser-thorne-the-red-team` — Ser Alliser Thorne: Master-at-Arms of Castle Black, a knight of the Night's Watch turned sworn brother to the Others' forces, the man who trained a generation of rangers to hate him
- `walder-frey-the-red-team` — Lord Walder Frey: Lord of the Crossing, head of House Frey, the Lord of the Twins, the man who waited through five conflicts and chose sides only when the winning stood to pay the highest price
- `rorge-the-red-team` — Rorge: Bandit, raider, murderer, and pit fighter — a man with no liege, no title, and no purpose beyond the next act of brutality, let loose on the Riverlands by the chaos of the War of the Five Kings

## House of the Dragon
- `rhaenyra-targaryen` — Rhaenyra Targaryen: Princess of Dragonstone, heir to the Iron Throne (disputed), Queen of the Seven Kingdoms (claimed during the Dance of the Dragons)
- `daemon-targaryen` — Daemon Targaryen: Prince of the Dragon, Lord of Flea Bottom (self-proclaimed), Commander of the City Watch, King of the Narrow Sea, Protector of the Realm (briefly), rider of Caraxes
- `alicent-hightower` — Alicent Hightower: Queen Consort of the Seven Kingdoms, mother to Aegon II, regent during the Dance of the Dragons, rider of no dragon but architect of the Green faction
- `otto-hightower` — Otto Hightower: Hand of the King to Viserys I, father of Queen Alicent, grandfather to Aegon II, architect of the Green faction throughout the Dance of the Dragons
- `viserys-i-targaryen` — Viserys I Targaryen: King of the Andals, the Rhoynar, and the First Men, Lord of the Seven Kingdoms, Protector of the Realm, rider of Balerion the Black Dread's younger brother Vhagar (formerly), then the younger dragon Syrax's rider's father
- `criston-cole-the-red-team` — Ser Criston Cole: Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, the Kingmaker, rider of no dragon but the swordsman who shaped the Dance of the Dragons through steel and oaths broken then remade
- `aemond-one-eye-the-red-team` — Aemond Targaryen: Prince of the Targaryen dynasty, rider of Vhagar (the largest and oldest living dragon), one-eyed warrior and dragonrider, commander of the Green aerial forces during the Dance
