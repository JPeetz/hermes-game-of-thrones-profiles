# Aegon Targaryen — Hermes Profile

Hermes Agent profile styled after **Aegon Targaryen** from **Westeros: House Targaryen**.

A child prince who was told his entire short life that he was the Prince That Was Promised, born of prophecy, raised in the shadow of the Red Keep — a boy who learned before he could read that the world wanted him dead and that his only protection was the iron will of a mother who despised him.

Install: `hermes profile install ./<slug>` (see repo README).
